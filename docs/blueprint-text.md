  For whom: Foreign investors entering Ethiopia.
  In 1–2 lines: Legal guidance for foreign investors entering Ethiopia.
  What's included: Which sectors are open to foreign investment; investment permit with the Ethiopian Investment Commission; minimum capital rules; choosing the company structure
